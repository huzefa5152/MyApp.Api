Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

This policy covers Trader Mailbox, the Gmail Email Workspace, and the FBR Digital ERP application operated by Huzefa Hussain. It explains how we handle information when you use the application or connect a Google account.

For privacy questions or a data-deletion request, contact huzefa.hussain1111@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We process account information, assigned roles and companies, business records you enter, and operational records needed to provide the service. Business records may include customer and supplier details, quotations, challans, invoices, payments, and tax information. We also record relevant access, synchronization, error and business-action events for operation, security and troubleshooting.

If you connect Gmail, we receive your Google account identifier and email address, OAuth authorization credentials, and the messages needed for the Email Workspace. Message information includes sender, subject, date, message and conversation identifiers, text or HTML content, and attachment metadata. Supported attachments are retrieved when you request a download, preview or extraction. Extracted information may become part of an enquiry draft or a business document you choose to create.

2. Gmail permissions and synchronization

Connecting Gmail is optional and requires your Google consent. The current release requests basic identity information and Gmail read-only access. It imports received messages from the initial 30-day window and then synchronizes new messages; spam, trash, drafts and sent messages are excluded by the current importer. The current release does not send Gmail replies or change Gmail read/unread status. Features needing additional permissions will require additional consent before use.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, synchronize connected mailboxes, display permitted enquiries, preview attachments, prepare quotation drafts, suggest catalogue matches and company-specific historical prices, and create business documents following your explicit actions. We also use operational information to maintain the service, investigate errors and enforce access controls.

Keeping, ignoring or restoring an enquiry changes its status in the application; it does not change the source email in Gmail. Quotation creation does not automatically email the quotation. Gmail message content and attachments are not sent to an external AI model by the current Email Workspace.

4. Company access and sharing

A company assignment does not give another user access to your entire Gmail mailbox. Email access is controlled by mailbox ownership, company assignments and application permissions. If you explicitly enable matching-email sharing for a company or keep an email as a company enquiry, permitted users of that company may access the corresponding enquiry. Separate company links and rules determine which enquiries are shared. Platform administration includes functions to manage access and remove company mailbox data.

We do not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising, credit decisions or training general-purpose AI models. Hosting and infrastructure providers support operation of the application. Google processes OAuth authorization and Gmail API requests. If you explicitly submit business information to FBR, that business information is sent to FBR as part of the invoicing service; connecting Gmail alone does not submit mail to FBR.

5. Google API Limited Use

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Human access to Google user data is limited to purposes permitted by that policy, such as access with your affirmative agreement for support, security investigations, legal obligations, or permitted internal operations.

6. Storage and security

Information is stored in the application's hosting environment and database. OAuth refresh tokens, cached message bodies and enquiry drafts are protected using the application's encryption mechanism. Operational message metadata, such as sender, subject and dates, is stored separately. Authentication, role permissions and company access checks restrict application access. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

Cached emails and enquiries are retained to support the connected workspace and associated business records. The current release does not automatically delete cached mail after a fixed retention period. Disconnecting or unlinking a company does not automatically erase existing enquiries, quotations or business records.

You may request deletion through the contact address above. We verify the requester's authority and relevant mailbox or company before acting. The platform's administrator can permanently remove a company mailbox link and its associated enquiry data; messages still referenced by another company are retained for that company. Previously created business documents and audit records may require separate handling or retention for accounting, security or legal obligations. Deleted information may remain in backups until those backups are rotated or removed. We will explain any applicable retention limitation when handling your request.

8. Disconnecting and revoking access

You can unlink a Gmail connection from the Email Workspace. If the mailbox has no remaining enabled company links, the application clears its active refresh token and attempts to revoke Google access. You can also revoke access directly through your Google Account's third-party connections. Revocation stops further authorized Gmail access but does not itself erase information previously stored in the application. Contact us if you also want stored data removed.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our data practices change and revise the effective date. Material changes involving additional Gmail permissions require the relevant Google consent. For questions, corrections or deletion requests, email huzefa.hussain1111@gmail.com.
